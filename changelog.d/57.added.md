**A BAI2 file from outside this project, and the tests that hold the mock to it**
(#57). `tests/samples/external/bai2-sample1.txt` comes from
[moov-io/bai2](https://github.com/moov-io/bai2) at a pinned commit, unmodified,
under the Apache License 2.0 already kept beside the other samples.
`tests/test_bai2_external.py` recomputes what it says - with its own eleven-line
parser, so that nothing asks `mockbank.bai2` whether `mockbank.bai2` is right.

It confirmed two readings the module had flagged as guesses: **record counts
include the trailer that states them** (a `49` covering 14 records says 14, and
counts `88` continuations among them) and **control totals are signed**, with a
`98` summing its accounts and a `99` its groups. It confirmed that the `03` names
an **account number** rather than an IBAN.

It corrected three things, each fixed where it was wrong rather than only in the
changelog: the `02`'s parties, the notation for a positive amount, and the reason
recorded for keeping movement totals off the `03`. That last one is worth stating
plainly - the reason given was that a summary would be counted twice, once in the
summary and again in each `16`. **That is exactly what BAI2 does**: each `49` in
the sample is precisely twice the sum of its own `16`s. Balances-only is still
what the mock writes, but as a choice rather than as an avoidance of a problem
that was not there.

It could not settle the two placeholder transaction codes, which stay marked in
`PLACEHOLDER_CODES` rather than being quietly promoted because the issue closed:
nothing in the file is an outgoing customer transfer or one coming back.

And it showed that **`mockbank.bai2.read` cannot read a real BAI2 file**, in
three ways - an undeclared `88`, and a value-dated funds type occupying three
fields where the declaration allows one, in both the `16` and the summary group,
where it escapes as a bare `ValueError` rather than a refusal. That is #114, and
each way is pinned by a passing test so it cannot be half-fixed or left to rot.
