**A BAI2 statement names its two parties the way a real BAI2 file does**
(#57). The `02` group header said `ultimate receiver` was the account's *name*
and `originator` its id - both the customer, with the bank named nowhere - so
every statement the mock wrote claimed the account had originated its own
statement. `moov-io/bai2`'s `sample1.txt`, now vendored as
`tests/samples/external/bai2-sample1.txt`, settles the direction: its `01` is
`sender=0004, receiver=12345` and its `02` is
`ultimate receiver=12345, originator=0004`, the bank first in one and second in
the other. **In BAI2 the originator of a group is the bank that produced the
file**, not whoever started a payment. The two parties are derived once now and
handed to both records, so they cannot disagree again.

**Balances and control totals carry an explicit sign.** They were written with a
`-` when negative and nothing when positive; the sample writes `+` on all four
of its control totals and both of its `03` balances, and writes no sign at all on
any movement amount. That is the line this module already drew between a balance
and a movement, so what changed is the notation: `49,+25125000,5/` where it used
to say `49,25125000,5/`. Not adopted is that sample's zero-padding to a fixed
width, which a comma-delimited format does not need and which it applies at three
different widths in one file.
